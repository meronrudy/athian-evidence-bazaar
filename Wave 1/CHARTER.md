# AgEvidence Open Source Design Advisory Board

## Purpose
The AgEvidence Open Source Design Advisory Board provides domain and product feedback on the public technical design of AgEvidence. The board helps ensure that AgEvidence's event contracts, evidence manifests, gap-analysis rules, review records and portable reliance artifacts reflect real agricultural technology workflows.

## Scope
Board participants may:
- review public company and product workflow hypotheses;
- correct terminology and system boundaries;
- identify interoperability and evidence gaps;
- review proposed event and source-record schemas;
- contribute synthetic examples;
- propose changes through issues and pull requests;
- participate in open technical design discussions;
- review documentation and reference implementations.

## Non-commercial status
Participation does not create:
- a customer or vendor relationship;
- a paid design-partner relationship;
- an obligation to purchase or deploy AgEvidence;
- an obligation to provide proprietary data;
- an endorsement of AgEvidence;
- an endorsement by AgEvidence of any participant;
- exclusivity;
- intellectual-property transfer beyond accepted open source contributions.

## Public-information boundary
Initial company workspaces are based on public information and are explicitly presented as unverified design hypotheses. A company may request corrections, attribution changes or removal of inaccurate representations at any time.

## Outputs
The board produces public open source assets:
- event-contract specifications;
- source-manifest formats;
- synthetic fixtures;
- evidence-gap taxonomies;
- human-review semantics;
- interoperability profiles;
- reliance-artifact specifications;
- reference implementations;
- architecture decisions.
