# Design Advisor Agreement

## Purpose
This agreement outlines the terms for participation as a Design Advisor in the Wave 1 Design-Advisor Workspace of AgEvidence.

## Role
As a Design Advisor, you will:
- Review and correct the proposed evidence flow for your company
- Identify commercially sensitive fields and boundaries
- Help define the minimum portable artifact that would make your product easier to sell into regulated, verified, financed, or Scope 3 programs
- Participate in two or three structured reviews
- Provide sample or synthetic field dictionaries

## Commitment
- Time: Approximately 2-3 hours spread over 2-3 structured reviews
- Output: Reviewable evidence contract and reliance artifact specification

## Deliverables from AgEvidence
- A company-specific evidence architecture developed at no initial charge
- A documented map of existing evidence and commercial assurance gaps
- Early influence over AgEvidence schemas and integration patterns
- A reusable artifact specification for buyer, verifier, program, or Scope 3 workflows
- Attribution as a design advisor (only with permission)
- Priority access to pilot implementation

## What We Do Not Require
- Transfer of proprietary models, customer lists, or raw datasets
- Exclusivity
- Claim that AgEvidence validates your company's climate performance

## Confidentiality
All feedback and corrections provided will be treated as confidential and used solely to improve the public workspace. No proprietary information will be made public without explicit permission.

## Term
This agreement is for the duration of the Wave 1 program (typically 4-6 weeks) and may be renewed for subsequent waves or converted to a formal design partnership.

## Acceptance
By participating in the structured reviews and providing feedback, you agree to the terms of this Design Advisor Agreement.

## Contact
For questions or to schedule reviews, please contact the Wave 1 program lead.
