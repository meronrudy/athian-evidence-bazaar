# Wave 1 Design-Advisor Workspace

## Program Charter
Wave 1 is a public design-partner program for agricultural technology companies whose operational systems already create commercially valuable evidence events. AgEvidence translates selected operational records into portable evidence contracts and independently verifiable reliance artifacts without replacing participating companies' products, data platforms, or customer relationships.

## Core Principles
- **Complementary Infrastructure**: AgEvidence does not replace existing systems; it connects their records into portable evidence graphs.
- **Company Control**: Participating companies retain full control of their product, data, and customer relationships.
- **Collaborative Draft**: The repository is a starting point for collaboration, not a claim of complete understanding.
- **Evidence-First Approach**: Focuses on making operational data inspectable, replayable, and institutionally defensible.

## Participation Process
1. Review the workspace and correct the proposed evidence flow
2. Identify commercially sensitive fields
3. Define the minimum portable artifact
4. Graduate to formal partnership

## Expected Outcomes
- Corrected workflow maps when a participating company provides substantive review
- Reviewable evidence-contract and reliance-artifact hypotheses
- Potential path to paid design partnership or implementation only after explicit commercial gates
