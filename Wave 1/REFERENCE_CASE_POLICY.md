# AgEvidence Open Source Design Advisory Board Reference Case Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how public-information reference cases are used in the AgEvidence Open Source Design Advisory Board. It ensures that company-specific workspaces are presented as unverified design hypotheses based on public information, without implying affiliation, endorsement, or commercial relationships.

## Scope
This policy applies to all company workspaces in the Event-First Systems Working Group, including but not limited to:
- DIT AgTech
- Agronomeye
- Agscent
- Cibo Labs
- MEQ Solutions

## Key Requirements
1. **Public Information Boundary**: All workspaces must be explicitly labeled as based on public information and unverified design hypotheses.
2. **Non-Affiliation Notice**: Each workspace must state that the company is not affiliated with AgEvidence.
3. **No Endorsement**: Participation does not imply endorsement or adoption of AgEvidence.
4. **Correction Process**: Companies may request corrections, attribution changes, or removal of inaccurate representations at any time.
5. **Synthetic Data**: Fixtures must be clearly marked as synthetic when real data is not publicly available.
6. **Attribution**: Contributions may be attributed only with explicit permission.

## Public Information Notice Template
```
> **Status:** Public-information design hypothesis  
> **Company participation:** Not confirmed  
> **Affiliation:** None  
> **Endorsement:** None  
> **Data:** Synthetic or publicly described only
```

## Compliance
Workspaces that do not meet these requirements may be requested to add the required notices or be removed from public view.
