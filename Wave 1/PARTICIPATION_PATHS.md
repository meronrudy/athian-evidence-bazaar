# Wave 1 Participation Paths

## Design Reviewer
**Company commitment:** One 30–45 minute review or asynchronous annotations
**AgEvidence commitment:** Correct the public workspace
**Output:** Validated workflow map

**Initial outreach:** "Would someone on your product, data, assurance, or commercial team review the workspace and tell me where the proposed evidence flow is wrong?"

## Design Advisor
**Company commitment:** Two or three structured reviews and sample/synthetic fields
**AgEvidence commitment:** Build a company-specific evidence contract
**Output:** Reviewable contract and artifact

## Design Partner
**Company commitment:** Named use case, technical contact, bounded test data
**AgEvidence commitment:** Implement and validate an integration slice
**Output:** Pilot-ready evidence bundle

## Conversion Gates

### Gate 0 — Prepared
- Public-information hypothesis complete
- One proposed evidence thread
- One artifact hypothesis
- Known uncertainties listed

### Gate 1 — Reviewed
- Company corrects terminology and workflow
- One internal owner identified
- Nonexistent events removed
- Sensitive boundaries documented

### Gate 2 — Advised
- Event contract reviewed
- Source-manifest route agreed
- Reliance language reviewed
- Sample or synthetic field dictionary available

### Gate 3 — Demonstrated
- Fixture passes schema validation
- Evidence gaps are projected
- Human review is recorded
- Artifact is generated
- Artifact verifies independently

### Gate 4 — Partnered
- Named commercial use case
- Named recipient or reliance party
- Bounded pilot scope
- Paid or formally sponsored implementation
- Success criteria and decision date

**Design advisor = Gate 2. Design partner = Gate 4.**
